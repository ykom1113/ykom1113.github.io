How to play

  • Slide your finger across the touch area below the game screen — the paddle moves to where you touch.
  • Tap the screen to launch the ball.
  • Clear every brick to finish the stage. There are 50 stages in all.
  • Catch the items that fall from broken bricks for temporary effects, such as a wider paddle or extra balls.

Frequently asked questions

I changed phones or reinstalled, and my purchases are gone

Open the shop and tap "Restore purchases". Max Paddle Start, Max Speed Start, Multi-Ball Start and Piercing Start are restored at no charge as long as you use the same Apple ID or Google account.

Stage Skip tickets are consumable items, so store rules mean they cannot be restored.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Not at the moment, sorry. Cleared stages and high scores are stored only on your device, and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

There's no sound

  1. Check that the speaker button at the bottom of the screen is not switched off.
  2. Check your device volume.
  3. If it is still silent, close the app completely and open it again.

On iOS, the game is set to play sound even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent.

Why do I see ads?

Ads are what let the game be free. One may appear after you clear a stage — from stage 3 onward, roughly once every few stages. Ads never interrupt you while you are playing.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in Japanese or English to match your device settings. You can switch at any time with the button at the bottom of the screen.

I would like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so the developer cannot issue refunds. Please use one of these instead:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play Help
