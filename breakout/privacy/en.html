Privacy Policy

Simple Breakout

日本語

Yusuke Komuro ("the Developer") provides the iOS and Android application Simple Breakout ("the App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information the Developer collects directly

The App does not ask you to create an account, and the Developer does not collect or transmit information that identifies you personally, such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address.

Your game data — cleared stages, high scores, trophies, purchased items, and your sound and language settings — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to a server operated by the Developer; the Developer operates no server at all.

Uninstalling the App deletes this data from your device.

2. Information collected by third-party services

So that the App can be offered free of charge, and so that in-app purchases can be processed, the App uses the third-party services listed below. Each of these may collect information in accordance with its own provider's privacy policy.

2-1. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure those ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device information such as device model, OS version, language and screen size
  • An approximate region derived from your IP address
  • Usage data such as ad impressions and taps

For how Google handles this information, please see Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google explains how it uses data for advertising here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2-2. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the App may show a dialog asking for permission to track. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used and you will see ads that are not based on your interests. Declining does not restrict any feature of the game.

You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

2-3. Opting out of personalised ads

  • iOS: turn off the tracking permission described above.
  • Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads to delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation.

2-4. In-app purchases

All payments for purchases made inside the App are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Payment details such as credit card numbers are never provided to the Developer. The Developer receives only the fact that a purchase completed.

3. Children's privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the Developer becomes aware that such information has been provided, it will be deleted promptly.

4. Disclosure to third parties

The Developer does not sell or otherwise provide information obtained from users to anyone other than the third-party services described in this policy, except where disclosure is required by law.

5. Your rights

Because the App's data is stored only on your device, the Developer cannot access, correct or delete it. To delete it, uninstall the App. For information collected in connection with advertising, please contact Google or use the opt-out steps in section 2-3 above.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as laws change or as features are added to the App. When it is updated, the content of this page and the revision date below will be changed.
